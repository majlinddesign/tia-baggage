57 airlines · 127 QR codes · links passenger-tested 17 Oct 2026 · 42 with online channels · 15 handled by Lost & Found manual PIR

Before the passenger leaves the baggage hall

  1. Report the missing or damaged bag at the Lost & Found / baggage services desk. The passenger receives a PIR with a file reference such as TIAW412345.
  2. Keep the boarding pass, bag tag and PIR. Photograph any damage.
  3. Online airlines: scan the QR code to report, track or claim. Manual PIR airlines: the Lost & Found team files the PIR and the airline is contacted.
  4. Written claim deadlines (Montreal Convention): damaged bags within 7 days, delayed bags within 21 days of delivery.
